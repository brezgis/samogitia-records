# Polish translation glossary: Plungė baptism registers, 1849–1907

> **Not reviewed by a native speaker of Polish.** Every Polish word choice below was made
> without a native reader. Section 10 lists the choices that most need checking, and
> section 4.7 says which place names were checked against sources and which were not.
> Translators: apply the rules as written anyway. Corrections will be made here first and
> then applied mechanically to the finished entries.

This glossary covers all five Plungė books (the same books and scribes as
[LT_GLOSSARY.md](LT_GLOSSARY.md)):

| Book | Years | Scribe's formula, in short |
|---|---|---|
| 1827/1/18 | 1849–1854 | "Альтаристомъ/Викарнымъ онаго Кс. …", "окрещенъ младенецъ / окрещена дѣвица", "урожденной [fem. form] [family, gen. pl.]", time of day ("по утру", "вечеромъ"), no townships |
| 1827/1/22 | 1867–1874 | "мѣстнымъ Кс. …", "окрещено дитя", "урожденной [fem. form, often nominative] [family, gen. pl.]", "сего 1869 года", scribe often writes а for о ("гада", "са всѣми") |
| 1886–1890 (book 1, folder plunge-1886-1890) | 1886–1890 | "Викарнымъ Кс: …", "[father] и [mother] изъ [maiden, gen. pl.] [family, gen. pl.]", "[X]ской волости" |

Every Polish translation uses these renderings, so a term is the same in every entry and
can be corrected everywhere at once. The register is the Polish of 19th-century metrical
books as a modern genealogist would write it: modern Polish spelling, the traditional
formula vocabulary (włościanie, prawowici małżonkowie, rodzice chrzestni, z domu …), and
dates in numerals. Written 2026-11-11, following the Lithuanian glossary of the same date
section for section, so that the two languages make the same decision for the same entry.

**Three principles behind every rule below.**

1. **Translate what is written; never restore what is not.** Do not reconstruct a "true"
   Polish or Lithuanian surname, place or feminine form. "Szymkus" stays Szymkus, not
   "Szymkiewicz"; "Kederawna" stays Kederawna, not "Kiedrówna". Where the glossary gives a
   mechanical rule, apply it even if you think you know the real name.
2. **One form per thing.** The same word, person, priest or place gets the same Polish form
   in every entry of a book. Where a choice exists, this glossary makes it. Do not choose
   differently.
3. **Polish alphabet only** in the sentence (a ą b c ć d e ę f g h i j k l ł m n ń o ó p r s
   ś t u w y z ź ż, and the digraphs ch, cz, dz, dż, rz, sz). Never Cyrillic, never
   Lithuanian letters (č, š, ž, ė, ū, į, ų), never "v", "x" or "q" in a name, never
   English "sh", "ch" for ч, "kh", "ts". **ł** appears only in given names (Michał,
   Stanisław, Paweł …), in the fixed place names (Płungiany, Płotele, Sałanty …) and in
   the fixed clergy forms (Szyłejko, Wołontowicz, Szołkowski …). A lay surname never gets
   ł: Russian л is always l in a lay surname.

Notation in the examples: "→" means "write". [?] marks an uncertain reading or a name
reduced mechanically. Square brackets also mark words that are not in the source (see
Placeholders).

## 1. Formula

The three fields are always built in this order. Every element is required. If the source
lacks an element, write it in square brackets in its place (e.g. "[rzymskokatolickim]",
"z dopełnieniem wszystkich obrzędów [Sakramentu]"). Only do this when the word is really
absent from a complete sentence. A word that is present but garbled ("таинстьа",
"Таиоства") is present.

**Baptism field.**
`Dnia [day] [month, gen.] [year] roku w płungiańskim rzymskokatolickim kościele parafialnym [priest phrase] ochrzcił niemowlę imieniem [NAME] z dopełnieniem wszystkich obrzędów Sakramentu.`

**Parents field (married parents).**
`[Estate of the family, gen. pl.], prawowitych małżonków [father, gen.] [family surname, gen.] i [mother, gen.] z domu [maiden name], syn/córka, urodzony/urodzona dnia [date] [place], [parish].`

**Godparents field.**
`Rodzicami chrzestnymi byli [status] [godfather] i [godmother][, panna / , wdowa / , żona [husband, gen.]].`

- "Dnia 15 czerwca 1868 roku w płungiańskim rzymskokatolickim kościele parafialnym miejscowy
  ksiądz Wojnowicz ochrzcił niemowlę imieniem Anna Barbara z dopełnieniem wszystkich
  obrzędów Sakramentu."
- "Włościan, prawowitych małżonków Ignacego Ostrowskiego i Anny z domu Nowicka, córka,
  urodzona dnia 15 czerwca 1868 roku w miasteczku Płungiany, tej parafii."
- "Rodzicami chrzestnymi byli Józef Nowicki i Barbara, żona Wincentego Sinkusa [?]."

## 2. Estates, status words and clergy ranks

### Estates and status of lay people

Estate words describe the whole family. They come first in the parents field, in the
genitive plural, and are followed by ", prawowitych małżonków" (Template A). Status words
that belong to the man alone (soldier, official) go in the genitive singular before the
father's name. ", prawowitych małżonków," then follows the mother's maiden name (Template
B), even if the scribe wrote the plural ("Отставныхъ Солдатовъ").

- Template A: "Włościan, prawowitych małżonków Jana Belawskiego [?] i Weroniki z domu Talimowna [?], córka, …"
- Template B: "Żołnierza rezerwy Józefa Szlożkusa [?] i Rozalii z domu Pstrawiczewna [?], prawowitych małżonków, córka, …"
- Both apply when the source has both ("Крестьянъ … волости отставнаго солдата …"): "Włościan gminy Płungiany, żołnierza dymisjonowanego Jana … i Anny z domu …, prawowitych małżonków, syn".

Godparents' status goes **before** each godparent's name, singular, as written:
"Rodzicami chrzestnymi byli włościanin Józef Stonkus i Anna Jankowska, panna". If the
source writes one plural status for both ("Крестьяне A и B"), write "Rodzicami chrzestnymi
byli włościanie A i B". Never "Rodzicami chrzestnymi, włościanami, byli …".

### Clergy ranks and order of words

Write "ksiądz" in full in the sentence. "ks." is used only in notes. "Кс." / "Кс:" /
"Ксендзомъ" / "Ксендзъ" = ksiądz. The words "онаго / оного / тамошнимъ / сего костела"
("of that/this church") are **not translated**: the church has just been named. Do not
write "tegoż kościoła" or "tego kościoła" for them. The priest is the subject of the
sentence, so his name is always in the **nominative** (the Russian instrumental "Яцевичемъ"
→ "Jacewicz").

## 3. Dates

- **Numerals always**, in every date of the sentence: "Dnia 13 kwietnia 1888 roku". Never
  number words ("trzydziestego pierwszego", "tysiąc osiemset"), never Roman numerals,
  never a mixture ("dwudziestego [siódmego?]"). No full stop after the day number.
- **Fixed order: day, month, year.** Write "dnia [day] [month, gen.] [year] roku" even when
  the source writes "Августа 5 дня 1849 года" or "1902 г. Января 18 дня" → "urodzony dnia 5
  sierpnia 1849 roku". Always "dnia" before the day and "roku" after the year (never "r."
  in the sentence; "18 I 1903 r." is only for notes). The baptism field starts "Dnia"
  (capital D); the birth date is "urodzony/urodzona dnia …".
- "сего 1869 года" → "1869 roku" ("сего" is not translated). "1902 г." → "1902 roku".
  "числа" → nothing extra ("5 числа Мая" → "dnia 5 maja").
- Months (gen.): Января stycznia, Февраля lutego, Марта marca, Апрѣля kwietnia, Мая maja,
  Іюня czerwca, Іюля lipca, Августа sierpnia, Сентября września, Октября października,
  Ноября listopada, Декабря grudnia. Misread month words must still be translated ("mada" =
  мая → maja; "Mara" = марта → marca; "Эюня", "Гюня", "Пюня" = Іюня → czerwca; "Поля",
  "Аюоля" = Іюля → lipca). A month heading in the margin ("Февраль", "Мѣсяцъ") is not part
  of the entry. Leave it out.
- Dates are never converted between the Julian and Gregorian calendars, and no calendar note
  is added.
- **Time of day** (mostly 1849–1854, after the birth date): по утру / поутру / утромъ →
  rano; вечеромъ → wieczorem; около полудня / въ полдень → około południa; ночью → w nocy.
  It is placed after "roku": "urodzona dnia 2 sierpnia 1849 roku rano we wsi Jazdajce [?],
  parafii płungiańskiej". Never "z rana", "rankiem".

### Placeholders (one vocabulary everywhere)

| Situation | Write | Example |
|---|---|---|
| one or more digits unreadable | [?] in place of the digits | "dnia 2[?] marca", "dnia [?]5 marca", "18[?]9 roku" |
| a number read but uncertain | number + [?] | "dnia 13 [?] marca" |
| a whole slot (day, month, year, name, word) unreadable | [nieczytelne] in that slot; "dnia"/"roku" kept | "dnia [nieczytelne] stycznia 1872 roku"; "dnia [nieczytelne] [nieczytelne] 1868 roku we wsi Narwojsze [?]" |
| the source does not give it | [nie podano] | "dnia [nie podano] sierpnia 1851 roku" |
| the line or page breaks off | [tekst się urywa] at the break | |
| a clear but impossible reading (birth after baptism, 1811 in an 1850 entry) | the number as read + [tak w oryginale] | "urodzony dnia 3 maja 1811 [tak w oryginale] roku …". Never "[sic]" or "[tak!]". If the reading itself is unsure, use [?] instead. |
| a formula word missing | the word in brackets | "z dopełnieniem wszystkich obrzędów [Sakramentu]" |

Notes and inserted remarks use Polish quotation marks „…”. Never leave Cyrillic in the
Polish sentence; put a Cyrillic reading only in the notes field.

## 4. Places

### 4.1 Place type words

In Polish the type word comes **first** and the name follows it **in the nominative**,
unchanged ("we wsi Lukniany [?]", "w miasteczku Płungiany"). Never decline the name ("we
wsi Luknianach", "w Płungianach") and never leave a Russian case ending on it.

| Russian | Polish |
|---|---|
| въ деревнѣ / деревни / дер. / д. X | we wsi X |
| въ мѣстечкѣ Плунгянахъ / въ М. Плунгянахъ / Мко Плунгянахъ | w miasteczku Płungiany |
| въ мѣстечкѣ X | w miasteczku X |
| въ городѣ X | w mieście X |
| въ Плунгянскомъ дворѣ / имѣніи Плунгяны | w majątku Płungiany |
| въ имѣніи X / во дворѣ X | w majątku X |
| въ фольваркѣ X | w folwarku X |
| въ застѣнкѣ X | w zaścianku X |
| въ околицѣ X | w okolicy X |
| на хуторѣ X | na chutorze X |

### 4.2 Parish after the place

| Russian | Polish |
|---|---|
| сего Прихода / здѣшняго прихода | tej parafii |
| того же Прихода / того-же / тогоже | tejże parafii |
| Плунгянскаго Прихода | parafii płungiańskiej |
| [X]скаго Прихода (another parish) | parafii [X], with X the place name in the nominative from 4.5: Кулевскаго → parafii Kule; Плотельскаго → parafii Płotele; Ретовскаго → parafii Retów |
| parish word missing | nothing is added, and nothing dangles. Not "we wsi X, parafii." |

The parish **follows** the place, after a comma, even where the 1849–1854 scribe writes it
first ("Плунгянскаго Прихода въ Деревни Яздайцяхъ" → "we wsi Jazdajce [?], parafii
płungiańskiej"). Never "w parafii", never "w tymże kościele".

### 4.3 How to form a village or manor name (mechanical)

The village name after "we wsi / w folwarku / w majątku" is a **nominative**. Follow these
steps:

1. If the place is in the fixed table (4.4 or 4.5), use that form, also for OCR variants of
   the same spelling.
2. If the source has a **plural locative** -ахъ / -яхъ (also misread -ах, -ак, -акъ, -ахь,
   -ян, -ach), remove it and add the Polish nominative plural ending. The ending depends
   only on the letter before it, so it invents nothing:

   | Russian ending | Polish ending | Examples |
   |---|---|---|
   | -ахъ after most consonants | **-y** | Кансудахъ → Kansudy [?]; Мардосахъ → Mardosy [?]; Лукнянахъ → Lukniany [?]; Кекштахъ → Kekszty [?] |
   | -ахъ / -яхъ after к, г | **-i** | Видейкахъ → Widejki [?]; Столгахъ → Stolgi [?]; Шаркяхъ → Szarki [?]; Труйкяхъ → Trujki [?] |
   | -цахъ / -цяхъ | **-ce** | Яздайцяхъ → Jazdajce [?]; Вайнайцяхъ → Wajnajce [?] |
   | -шахъ / -жахъ / -чахъ / -щахъ (and -шяхъ …) | **-sze / -że / -cze / -szcze** | Нарвойшахъ → Narwojsze [?] |
   | -ляхъ | **-le** | Пруселяхъ → Prusele [?]; Сурбляхъ → Surble [?] |

**Further rules for men.**
- The family surname goes **with the father**: "prawowitych małżonków Kazimierza
  Wolowskiego i Petroneli z domu Abrutis, syn". It never goes after the mother or after
  "z domu".
- -скій never stays "-skij" or "-skii"; -ичъ never becomes "-icz" + Russian ending
  ("-iczow", "-icza" in the nominative).
- Never turn a Lithuanian-type surname into a Polish one: Szymkus, not "Szymkiewicz";
  Ramonas, not "Ramonowicz"; Możejkis, not "Możejko"; and never drop -as/-is/-us
  ("Szymk", "Ramon").
- Husband or father named in a woman's description: the surname is that man's, in the
  Polish genitive by the steps above: "съ Анною Игнатія Тимнскаго супругою" → "Anna, żona
  Ignacego Timińskiego [?]"; "съ Теодорою Виктора Стонкуса супругою" → "Teodora, żona
  Wiktora Stonkusa". Never "-ago", "-ogo", "-aja".

| -цкая / -цкою / -цкаго (misread) | -cka: Новицкою → Nowicka |
| -овна / -овны / -овной / -овною / -евна / -евною / -ювна / -івна / -ювною | -owna / -ewna / -iowna (never -ówna): Криповною → Kripowna; Шакевною → Szakewna; Симцутовною → Simcutowna [?] |

| any other -ая / -ою / -ей / -ею | -a: -ая → -a, never -aja |
| a masculine-looking form actually written for the woman (Шакисъ, Стонкусъ, Бурмистровичъ) | as written, unchanged in every case: Szakis, Stonkus, Burmistrowicz [?] |

A woman's name that the draft gives with a Russian genitive -аго / -ого (book 22: "Аннаго

that man's surname, in the Polish genitive (7.2): "Anna, żona Ignacego Timińskiego"; "Anna,
żona Nowickiego". Only a -аго form directly on the woman's own name, with no husband or
father in the phrase (typically before "дѣвицею"), is the misread -ою.

### 7.4 Maiden names after "z domu"

"z domu" is followed only by the maiden name, with no comma before it ("Anny z domu
Nowicka"). There are two cases, decided by how the register writes the maiden name.

**Case 1: written as a family genitive plural** ("изъ Мокусовъ", "изъ Якубовскихъ",

- **(1a) Slavic-suffix base certain** (-скихъ, -цкихъ, -ичей / -ичевъ / -ичовъ): "z domu" +

- 1886–1907: "[father] и [mother] изъ/урожд. [maiden] [family] законныхъ супруговъ". The

- 1849–1874: "[father] и [mother] урожденной [maiden, fem.] [family, gen. pl.]". Same order.
- Template: "[estate], prawowitych małżonków [father, gen.] [family, gen.] i [mother, gen.]
  z domu [maiden], syn/córka, urodzony/urodzona …". Example: "Włościan gminy Płungiany,
  prawowitych małżonków Józefa Knizelisa i Petroneli z domu Możejkis, córka, urodzona dnia
  18 sierpnia 1896 roku …".
- Unmarried mother: no "z domu" (see Special cases).

|---|---|---|
| nominative | Józef, syn Stanisława, Griczus | Anna, córka Antoniego, Gekusowna [?] |
| genitive | Józefa, syna Stanisława, Griczusa | Anny, córki Antoniego, Gekusowny [?] |

- Source: "Крестьянъ Жоранской волости Іосифа Станиславова и Варвары урожденной Януневной
  Гричусовъ" → "Włościan gminy Żorany [?], prawowitych małżonków Józefa, syna Stanisława,
  Griczusa [?] i Barbary z domu Janunewna [?], syn". (Гричусовъ has a written -ус ending,
  so it declines; the [?] is the reading.)
- The father's given name is taken from the patronymic stem and given its glossary form:
  Станиславовъ / Станиславова → Stanisława; Іосифовъ → Józefa; Антоновъ / Антоновна →
  Antoniego; Михайловъ → Michała; Казимировъ → Kazimierza; Игнатьевъ → Ignacego;
  Августиновъ → Augustyna; Іоановна → Jana; Даниловъ → Daniela.
- Never "-owicz" made from a patronymic ("Józef Stanisławowicz"), never "Józefa
  Stanisławowa", never parentheses ("Alberta (syna Daniela) Borana"), never without commas.
- How to tell a patronymic from a surname: a word in -овъ / -ова / -овичъ / -овна / -евна is
  a patronymic **only if** its stem is a given name in the table **and** another surname
  follows it. If only one word follows the given name, it is the surname ("Варвары
  Петровичевны" → "Barbary Petrowiczewny").

### 7.6 Locative after "wdowa po" (only use of the locative)

"[B] вдовою [husband]" → "[B], wdowa po [husband, locative]". Given names: Jan → Janie,
Józef → Józefie, Antoni → Antonim, Ignacy → Ignacym, Wincenty → Wincentym, Jerzy → Jerzym,
Kazimierz → Kazimierzu, Tomasz → Tomaszu, Mateusz → Mateuszu, Andrzej → Andrzeju,
Franciszek → Franciszku, Michał → Michale, Paweł → Pawle, Piotr → Piotrze, Kacper →
Kacprze, Wawrzyniec → Wawrzyńcu, Stanisław → Stanisławie, Jakub → Jakubie, Szymon →
Szymonie, Marcin → Marcinie, Adam → Adamie; a name not in this list: write it in the
nominative + [?]. Surnames: -ski → -skim, -cki → -ckim, -icz → -iczu, -as → -asie, -is →
-isie, -us → -usie, -ius → -iusie, consonant-final → as in the genitive rule, the locative
of ordinary Polish nouns (Widmont → Widmoncie); if unsure, or for -a, -o and [?] bases,
write the surname unchanged. Example: "Malwina, wdowa po Janie Donowskim"; "Anna, wdowa po
Józefie Stupel [?]".

## 8. Special cases

### 8.1 Twins

- Both twins in one entry ("окрещены близнецы", "близнята родившіеся"):
  - baptism: "ochrzcił niemowlęta bliźnięta imionami Józef i Kazimierz" (never "imieniem A"
    twice, never "bliźniaków");
  - parents: two boys "synowie (bliźnięta), urodzeni dnia …"; a boy and a girl "syn i córka
    (bliźnięta), urodzeni dnia …"; two girls "córki (bliźnięta), urodzone dnia …". Always
    nominative, never "dzieci (bliźnięta)";
  - godparents, per child, in the order of the names: "Rodzicami chrzestnymi pierwszego
    niemowlęcia byli włościanin Marcin Otunowicz [?] i [nieczytelne], żona włościanina;
    rodzicami chrzestnymi drugiego niemowlęcia byli włościanin Wincenty Amim [?] i
    Petronela, żona włościanina Lawrynta [?] Iundwi [?]."
- One twin per entry ("сынъ изъ близнецевъ", "дочь изъ близнецевъ"): baptism as normal
  ("ochrzcił niemowlę imieniem …"); parents "syn, jeden z bliźniąt, urodzony …" / "córka,
  jedna z bliźniąt, urodzona …". Never "syn z bliźniąt", "(bliźnię)".

### 8.2 Illegitimate births

| Source | Polish |

| Крестьянки Анны Видейковны незаконнорожденный сынъ | Nieślubny syn włościanki Anny Widejkowny, urodzony … |
| Крестьянки Плунгянской волости внѣбрачной Петронели Касперовичевной дочь | Nieślubna córka włościanki gminy Płungiany Petroneli Kasperowiczewny, urodzona … |
| Крестьянки Плунгямской Вол Евы Тарвидовны незаконнорожденный сынъ | Nieślubny syn włościanki gminy Płungiany Ewy Tarwidowny, urodzony … |
| Крестьянки … внѣбрачной Стани славы Вилновной дѣвицы сынъ | Nieślubny syn włościanki gminy Bernatow [?] panny Stanisławy Wilnowny [?], urodzony … |
| Крестьянки … Варвары Іміяновны Войчеховской дѣвицы дочь | Nieślubna córka włościanki … panny Barbary, córki Imijana [?], Wojczechowskiej, urodzona … |

| незаконнорожденный сынъ, mother not named | Nieślubny syn, urodzony … |

Rules: the field starts with "Nieślubny syn" / "Nieślubna córka". Then the mother's
estate in the genitive singular ("włościanki", never "włościan" for one mother), then the
township if written, then "panny" (дѣвицы) or "wdowy" (вдовы), then her names. Everything
about the mother is genitive (given name, patronymic construction, surname by 7.3).
"незаконнорожденный", "внѣбрачный" and "незаконный" all → nieślubny / nieślubna. Never
"z nieprawego łoża", never "naturalny".

### 8.3 Foundlings

"Подкидышъ неизвѣстныхъ родителей дочь, найденная 5 Августа 1904 года въ М. Плунгянахъ
того же прихода" → "Podrzutek, córka nieznanych rodziców, znaleziona dnia 5 sierpnia 1904
roku w miasteczku Płungiany, tejże parafii." Boy: "Podrzutek, syn nieznanych rodziców,
znaleziony …". Only "Podrzutek" (for both sexes), never "znajda", "podrzutka".

### 8.4 Emergency baptism ("съ воды") and supplied ceremonies

- "окрещенъ одною водою / съ воды / изъ воды" (baptised with water only, usually at home):
  "[date] … [person] ochrzcił z wody niemowlę imieniem X." The phrase "z dopełnieniem
  wszystkich obrzędów Sakramentu" is then **not** written, because the source says the
  opposite. If a person and place are given ("окрещенъ дома повивальною бабкою"), translate
  them as written ("ochrzciła z wody w domu akuszerka" is the one case where the verb is
  feminine).
- "дополнены обряды Крещенія / дополнено Крещеніе надъ [N]-лѣтнимъ дитятею по имени X"
  (rites supplied in church for a child already baptised with water): "Dnia … w
  płungiańskim rzymskokatolickim kościele parafialnym [priest] dopełnił wszystkich
  obrzędów Sakramentu nad [N]-letnim niemowlęciem imieniem X." The age is written only if
  the source gives it. If "Таинства" is absent: "dopełnił wszystkich obrzędów [Sakramentu]
  nad …".
- The printed 1849 form question ("одною водою или со всѣми обрядами окрещенъ?") is the
  form's heading, not an entry. See 8.7.

### 8.5 Entries from or about another parish

- Child born in another parish: the place and "parafii [X]" as written: "… urodzony dnia 4
  lutego 1869 roku we wsi Pokutowiany [?], parafii Kule."
- Baptism by another parish's priest: "ksiądz proboszcz parafii [X] [name]" in the priest
  slot. His name follows the clergy rule for unlisted priests, + [?].
- Baptism performed elsewhere and copied into this book (another church named in the
  baptism sentence): write that church by 4.6 ("w rzymskokatolickim kościele parafialnym
  parafii Kule") in place of the Plungė church. Keep the formula and its order.
- Parents from another parish or district are named with that unit after the estate:
  "Szlachciców powiatu rosieńskiego, …", "Mieszczan miasta Telsze, …".

### 8.6 Rows with several entries

Some rows (one image line) hold two or more entries. In **each** field, number them "(1) …
(2) …" in the same order, and repeat the **full** formula in every entry. That includes the
full date, "w płungiańskim rzymskokatolickim kościele parafialnym", the priest and "z
dopełnieniem wszystkich obrzędów Sakramentu". Never write "w tymże kościele", "tamże", "tenże
ksiądz" for the church or priest, and no headers such as "Trzy wpisy.". Godparents: "(1)
Rodzicami chrzestnymi byli … (2) Rodzicami chrzestnymi byli …". If one entry of the row is
a word-for-word copy of the previous one, translate it as written and say so in the notes.

### 8.7 Printed blank forms and margin notes

A row that contains only the printed heading of the 1849 form ("Тысяча … Н. … сынъ или дочь,
родившійся или родившаяся … поутру, или около полудня, или вечеромъ") is not an entry.
Write "[Tekst niewypełnionego drukowanego formularza.]" in each field. Month headings, page
numbers and later margin remarks (including the margin surname) are not part of the entry;
mention them only in the notes.

## 9. Checklist before returning (the ten errors to look for)

1. **Village after "we wsi", in the nominative, by 4.3/4.4**: "we wsi Lukniany [?]", never
   "we wsi Luknianach", "w Luknianach", "we wsi Luknianų" or "Lukniany wieś". Same village,
   same form (table 4.4).
2. **"ochrzcił niemowlę imieniem"** for every child; "imieniem" for one child (even with two
   names); "niemowlęta bliźnięta imionami" only for twins. No passive "ochrzczony".
3. **Dates in numerals, day–month–year, "dnia … roku"**: "urodzony dnia 7 kwietnia 1904
   roku". Never number words, "r." or Roman numerals in the sentence.
4. **Formula complete**: "w płungiańskim rzymskokatolickim kościele parafialnym", the
   priest, "ochrzcił niemowlę imieniem X", "z dopełnieniem wszystkich obrzędów Sakramentu",
   in that order. Missing words go in brackets; "w tymże kościele" is never used.
5. **Clergy**: exactly the form in the book's list (Szyłejko, Markiewicz, Mongird,
   Wojnowicz; Pacewicz, not Jacewicz, in 1867–1874; Baltruken, Paszkanis, Bajorinas,
   Bublis, Jarulajtis), "ksiądz" written out, nominative, rank order as in section 2.
6. **Maiden name after "z domu"**: Case 1a Polish genitive plural (z domu Jakubowskich,
   z domu Kasperowiczów); Case 1b nominative base (z domu Mokus, z domu Tarwid [?]); Case 2
   written feminine form in the nominative (z domu Kederawna, z domu Orlowska). Never
   -skaja, never -ówna, never -ski for -ская.
7. **No Russian endings and no restoration on surnames**: no -ow/-ew/-owa from a Russian
   genitive plural, no -ago/-ogo, -aja, -skij, -iczow. Ambiguous genitive plurals become stem
   + [?] (Stupel [?]), undeclined. Lithuanian-type names keep -as/-is/-us and decline
   (Szymkusa); they never become -owicz or -ski. The family surname is with the father.
8. **Women in genitive phrases**: the whole name is genitive ("włościanki Barbary
   Petrowiczewny", "Antoniny Czeblowskiej"). Never a masculine form for a woman. Godmothers:
   -ska / -owna in the nominative, ", panna" after the name.
9. **Given names from the table**: Jan (Иванъ, Янъ), Wincenty (Викентій), Józef (Осипъ,
   Іосифъ), Franciszek (Францъ), Ignacy / Ignacego, Anna (Ганна), Ewa, Barbara (Варвара),
   Dominik, Domicela, Petronela, Mateusz, Kacper, Wawrzyniec, Konstanty.
10. **Polish letters only and no untranslated Russian**: no Cyrillic, no Lithuanian č/š/ž/ė,
    no v, sh, kh, ts; no ł in a lay surname or unlisted village; e not "ie" (Kederawna,
    Belawski); рж → rż in lay names. No leftover Russian words ("odnosemi", "mada",
    "zdginiago"). Patronymics as "Józefa, syna Stanisława, Griczusa". Status singular for a
    soldier father ("Żołnierza rezerwy …, prawowitych małżonków, córka"). No "(gmina nie
    podana)" before 1861.

## 10. Open questions for a native reader

Nothing here should be changed by translators. These are points a native speaker of Polish
or a historian should confirm. Changing any of them later would be a mechanical second pass.

**Polish usage (not verified; flagged rather than guessed)**

1. **The baptism formula** "Dnia … w płungiańskim rzymskokatolickim kościele parafialnym
   ksiądz wikary X ochrzcił niemowlę imieniem Y z dopełnieniem wszystkich obrzędów
   Sakramentu." Is the word order natural? Is "Sakramentu" with a capital S right, or
   should it be "sakramentu" / "Sakramentu Chrztu"? Is "imieniem" without a comma right?
2. **"niemowlę" for every child**, including a child of several years whose rites are
   supplied ("nad 3-letnim niemowlęciem"), and "niemowlęta bliźnięta imionami" for twins.
   "dziecię" may be better in the supplied-rites case.
3. **"prawowitych małżonków"** (vs "z prawego małżeństwa", "ślubnych małżonków"), and its
   position in Templates A and B.
4. **"Rodzicami chrzestnymi byli"** (vs "Chrzestnymi byli"), "Ojcem chrzestnym był",
   "Matką chrzestną była".
5. **"z domu" + nominative** in Case 2 and 1b ("Anny z domu Kederawna", "z domu Mokus")
   against "z domu" + genitive plural in Case 1a ("z domu Jakubowskich"). Both patterns
   occur in Polish genealogy, but the mixture may read oddly. Should Case 1b rather be "z
   domu Mokusów" (Polish genitive plural of the certain base)? The present rule was chosen
   because it gives the same base as the Lithuanian glossary.
6. **Estate and status terms** chosen without confirmation: "szlachcic" (дворянинъ) vs
   "szlachcic zagrodowy" (шляхтичъ); "jednodworzec / jednodworka" (однодворецъ);
   "obywatel" (гражданинъ, perhaps "mieszczanin" was meant); "człowiek wolny / kobieta
   wolna"; "żołnierz dymisjonowany" (отставной; "żołnierz w stanie spoczynku" or "żołnierz
   zwolniony ze służby" are alternatives); "żołnierz urlopowany bezterminowo"; "żona
   żołnierza" for солдатка (the old Polish "żołnierka" was avoided because it now means a
   female soldier); "przystaw stanowy" (or "pristaw"); "radca dworu"; "sekretarz
   kolegialny"; "ziemianin" (помѣщикъ; "obywatel ziemski" is the period term).
7. **"gmina" for волость** (vs "włość"), and the noun construction "gminy Płungiany"
   instead of an adjective ("gminy płungiańskiej"). The noun was chosen so that unlisted
   townships can be written mechanically (Bernatow [?]) without inventing a Polish adjective.
   For the same reason parishes other than Plungė are "parafii Kule", not "parafii
   kulskiej".
8. **Place type words**: "w okolicy X" for околица (in modern Polish "okolica" also means
   "neighbourhood", so "w okolicy X" can be misread as "near X"; "w okolicy szlacheckiej X"
   would be clearer but adds words); "w zaścianku"; "na chutorze"; "w majątku" for
   двор/имѣніе.
9. **Village plurals** (4.3): -ахъ → -y/-i, -яхъ → -le/-nie/-ry/-ie, -цяхъ → -ce. These are
   mechanical Polish nominative plurals of the Russian spellings, not gazetteer names. Many
   villages surely have historical Polish names in SGKP (Pokutowiany? Kowszany? Prusele?);
   a later pass could replace them.
10. **Declension of -o and consonant-final men's surnames** ("Lawrinki", "Możejki",
    "Widmonta", "Jundula"). Polish grammar declines them, but genealogical practice sometimes
    leaves foreign surnames undeclined. The Lithuanian glossary leaves them undeclined.
11. **Women's surnames that are not Polish feminine forms** (Stonkus, Szakis) are left
    undeclined, which is standard Polish; please confirm that "-owny" / "-ewny" is the
    right genitive of -owna / -ewna written without ó.
12. **"wdowa po" + locative** (7.6): the locative list for given names and surname endings,
    especially for Lithuanian-type surnames ("po Józefie Szymkusie").
13. **Clergy ranks**: "ksiądz dziekan X", "ksiądz proboszcz dziekan X", "miejscowy ksiądz
    wikary X", "ksiądz dziekan retowski X", and "ksiądz filialista X" for Филіальнымъ (the
    least certain term). Also whether a deacon written "Кс." should still be only "diakon".
14. **Twins**: "synowie (bliźnięta)", "córki (bliźnięta)", and "Rodzicami chrzestnymi
    pierwszego niemowlęcia byli …; rodzicami chrzestnymi drugiego niemowlęcia byli …".
15. **Patronymic construction** "Józefa, syna Stanisława, Griczusa": natural, or is
    "Józefa Griczusa, syna Stanisława" preferable?
16. **"Podrzutek"** for a girl as well as a boy (the feminine "podrzutka" exists but is
    rare); and "Nieślubny syn" at the start of the field.
17. **Given names**: Валентинъ merged with Валентій as Walenty (as in the Lithuanian
    glossary; Walentyn is a separate Polish name); Каспаръ → Kacper (vs Kasper); Грасильда →
    Grasylda; Домитилля → Domitylla; Мадеушъ read as Тадеушъ → Tadeusz [?] (could also be
    Матеушъ → Mateusz); Симеонъ → Symeon.
18. **Letter rules that deliberately do not Polonise**: е always e (Kederawna, Belawski,
    Mickewicz, not "Kiedrawna", "Bielawski", "Mickiewicz"); рж → rż (Andrżejewski, not
    Andrzejewski); л always l. Decision 3 of the brief fixed these. A native reader may
    want -кевичъ → -kiewicz and рж → rz for Slavic-suffix names, since Russian clerks
    wrote those letters precisely to render Polish "kie" and "rz". The one Polish
    spelling rule applied is ń before -sk- (Jasiński, Kuczyński), because Polish never
    writes "-inski".
19. **Clergy forms**: Kirszyn [?] for Киршинъ (the Lithuanian glossary writes Kiršinas [?]
    with an -as the Russian does not have); Jarulajtis and Jurgajtis (Polish j for й) where
    the Lithuanian glossary writes Jarulaitis, Jurgaitis; Masilijanis [?] (Lithuanian
    glossary Masilianis [?]). The open clergy questions of the Lithuanian glossary (Baltruken,
    Bajorinas, Meritowicz [?], Kierpowski, Kerwel as one priest or two) apply here too.

**Place names and terms that could not be verified**

20. **Kartena → Korciany.** The brief proposed "Kartena". The historical Polish name is
    Korciany (Polish Wikipedia, citing SGKP t. IV s. 392 and t. XV cz. 2 s. 122), which is
    also what the Russian "Корцяны" renders, so this glossary uses Korciany. Korciany is not
    in the official KSNG list, and the SGKP page itself was not read.
21. **Olsiady, Żorany, Kule** are not in the official KSNG list. Olsiady is confirmed by the
    SGKP cross-reference "Alsiedy, ob. Olsiady" (vol. 1, Wikisource). Żorany and Kule rest on
    Polish Wikipedia citing SGKP (t. XIV s. 830; t. IV s. 860); the SGKP pages were not read.
22. **The identification of the townships** Жоранская = Żorany and Корцянская = Korciany
    keeps its [?], as in the Lithuanian glossary. Бернатовская (Bernatow [?]), Гинтеминская
    (Gintemin [?]) and Лабардзевская (Labardzew [?]) are not identified.
23. **Adjectives**: płungiański and retowski are verified (KSNG district names); telszewski,
    szawelski, kowieński and rossieński are verified in SGKP (the modern spelling rosieński
    is used, from KSNG "rejon rosieński"). No other adjective was verified, which is why the
    glossary uses nouns elsewhere.
24. **Period vocabulary** (włościanin, jednodworzec, przystaw, radca dworu, folwark, zaścianek,
    okolica, chutor) was not checked against a period source such as a Polish-language
    metrical book from the Kovno governorate.
